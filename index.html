EA600 • County Road Trips

One loop.
Four counties.
14 days of discovery.

The EA600 is a 600-mile circular journey from Cambridge through the Fens, along the whole of the Norfolk and Suffolk coast, and home through Essex — linking historic towns, wild landscapes, seaside roads and the stories that make East Anglia distinctive.

A road trip designed to be explored slowly.

Fourteen days gives the EA600 enough space for the major landmarks — but also for the detours, village stops, coastal walks, independent food, gardens, museums and hidden places that turn a route into a journey.
