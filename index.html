Everything you need before you go.

Practical notes from our route team — the essentials for planning a relaxed, well-paced week on the EA600.

When to Go

May through September is ideal for long evenings and open attractions. Spring and autumn are quieter on the roads.

Where to Stay

Coaching inns, harbour cottages and country-house hotels in each overnight town.

Driving Information

Mostly A and B roads with single-track coastal lanes. Drive on the left.

Parking

Pay-and-display in most towns. National Trust members park free at many sites.

Food & Dining

Cromer crab, Brancaster mussels, Adnams ale and proper Sunday roasts.

What to Pack

Layers for coastal wind, walking boots and something for the beach.

Useful Information

Emergency 999. Many village shops close early and on Sundays.

Trip Duration

Seven days is ideal. Five is possible; ten lets you slow right down.
